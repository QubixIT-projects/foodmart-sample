Who We Are

From a retail counter to a food supply partner.

Ayngara Enterprises began with a simple purpose: make good food ingredients and FMCG products easy to access. That purpose now shapes two connected businesses - Ayngara Enterprises, our trading and distribution company, and Ayngara Food Mart, the retail outlet where individual customers shop.

Our long-term direction is wholesale, distribution, sourcing and importing - supplying the businesses that feed Sri Lanka every day: hotels, resorts, restaurants, cafés, bakeries, caterers and retailers. We built our operations, and this website, to scale with that direction.

01

Reliability businesses can plan around

Consistent stock, scheduled delivery and pricing that scales with order volume - the fundamentals a commercial kitchen depends on.

02

Sourcing with intent

We select ingredients, FMCG and imported products for consistency of quality, not just lowest cost — because our customers' reputations depend on it too.

03

A partner, not just a supplier

From a single café to a hotel kitchen, we work directly with food professionals to understand what keeps their service running.

04

Built to scale

Our catalogue, sourcing network and operations are structured to expand into wholesale, distribution and importing as demand grows.
